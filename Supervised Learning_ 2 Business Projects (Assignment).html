Supervised learning: 2 business projects

Choose a project below, download its CSV file, and complete the tasks in a Jupyter Notebook or Google Colab. Submit your notebook with a short business report. Both projects are written for beginners.

Project 1: Predict which customers will default on a loan

  • Classification
  • 1000 rows
  • 12 columns
  • Target: default

Business problem

A small finance company gives personal loans. About 1 in 7 customers fails to repay, and each default costs the company money. The manager wants a model that flags risky applicants before a loan is approved, and wants to know which factors matter most.

Your goal

Build a model that predicts default (1 = customer defaulted, 0 = repaid) and recommend how the company should use it.

Dataset

ColumnMeaning
loan_idUnique ID (do not use as a feature)
ageCustomer age in years
genderMale or Female
monthly_incomeMonthly income in rupees (some values missing)
employment_typeSalaried, Self-employed, Business or Student
loan_amountLoan amount in rupees
loan_term_monthsRepayment period in months
credit_scoreScore from 300 to 900 (some values missing)
existing_loansNumber of other active loans
missed_payments_last_yearMissed payments in the last 12 months
city_tierMetro, Tier-2 or Tier-3
defaultTarget. 1 = defaulted, 0 = repaid

First 8 rows

Tasks

  1. Load the CSV and check its shape, data types and summary statistics.
  2. Find the missing values and fill them (for example with the median). Explain your choice.
  3. Explore the data: default rate overall, and by employment type and missed payments. Make at least 3 charts.
  4. Convert text columns to numbers (one-hot encoding). Split the data 80% train and 20% test, keeping the default ratio the same in both.
  5. Train Logistic Regression and Decision Tree. Random Forest is a bonus.
  6. Evaluate on the test set: confusion matrix, precision, recall, F1-score and ROC-AUC. Do not rely on accuracy alone, because only about 15% of customers default.
  7. Write 5 to 8 lines for the manager: the best model, the top 3 risk factors, and what happens if the approval threshold is lowered.

Starter code

import pandas as pd
df = pd.read_csv("loan_default_dataset.csv")
print(df.shape)
print(df.isna().sum())
print(df["default"].value_counts(normalize=True))

Project 2: Predict the selling price of a flat

  • Regression
  • 800 rows
  • 11 columns
  • Target: price_lakhs

Business problem

A real estate agency wants to quote fair prices quickly. Today agents guess prices from experience, so quotes vary a lot. The agency wants a model that estimates a flat's price from its details and shows which features raise the price most.

Your goal

Build a model that predicts price_lakhs (price in lakh rupees) and judge how reliable its estimates are.

Dataset

ColumnMeaning
house_idUnique ID (do not use as a feature)
zoneCentral, IT Corridor, East, West, North or Suburb
area_sqftBuilt-up area in square feet
bedroomsNumber of bedrooms (1 to 4)
bathroomsNumber of bathrooms
age_yearsAge of the building (some values missing)
floorFloor number
parking_slotsParking slots (0 to 2)
furnishingUnfurnished, Semi-furnished or Furnished
distance_to_it_park_kmDistance to the nearest IT park in km
price_lakhsTarget. Price in lakh rupees (a few extreme values are included)

First 8 rows

Tasks

  1. Load the CSV and check its shape, data types and summary statistics.
  2. Fill the missing values in age_years. Find the 4 or so extreme prices with a box plot and decide whether to remove them. Explain why.
  3. Explore: price vs area (scatter plot), average price by zone, and a correlation heatmap.
  4. One-hot encode zone and furnishing. Split the data 80% train and 20% test.
  5. Train Linear Regression and Decision Tree Regressor. Random Forest is a bonus.
  6. Evaluate on the test set with MAE, RMSE and R². Say in plain words what the MAE means in lakhs.
  7. Predict the price of a new 2-bedroom, 950 sq ft flat in any zone, and list the 3 features that matter most.

Starter code

import pandas as pd

print(df["price_lakhs"].describe())
